# 01 — Brand Strategy and Commercial Positioning

> Kaynak: Kullanıcının ZIP'i / docs/01-brand-strategy.md. Güncel durum ve bu turdaki kapsam için [[07 - AI Handoff]] ve [[08 - Phase 1 Tasarım Önerisi]] okunur. Mevcut Obsidian kasası kullanıcı kararıyla bayes 2.0/ konumunda korunur.


## Confirmed direction
BayesSoft is a software engineering company serving external clients now, with long-term plans to develop its own SaaS products. The site should **lead with real existing service capability**, not give the misleading impression of a released product portfolio.

## Today's navigation (proposal)
`Ana Sayfa | Hizmetler | Projeler | Hakkımızda | İletişim` with a prominent `Projenizi Konuşalım` CTA. Do not add `Ürünler`, `SaaS` or `BayesSoft Products` navigation until an actual ownable/commercial product or actionable launch program exists and the user approves it.

## Distinction that matters
- **Website building / bespoke client work = service**, not SaaS.
- **Building a SaaS platform *for a client* = client project**, not automatically BayesSoft's owned SaaS.
- **BayesSoft-owned recurring-license software offered to many clients = BayesSoft SaaS product.**
- Do not suggest BayesSoft owns IP, data or rights from client work unless contractually confirmed.

## Brand voice
Professional, direct, approachable; less generic tech jargon. Focus first on customer problem and solution, then optional technical depth. Avoid unsupported enterprise-scale claims. Five-person team is a credible authentic differentiator. Emphasize end-to-end delivery and sustained maintainability.

## Candidate hero copy (NOT finally approved)
- `Yazılım geliştiriyoruz. Dijital ürünler inşa ediyoruz.` — proposed during hybrid stage; can imply own products; treat carefully until actual product launch.
- `Dijital fikirleri güçlü yazılımlara dönüştürüyoruz.` — later Phase 1 design mockup, fits current service business.
- Alternative: `İşletmeniz için güçlü web, mobil ve yapay zekâ çözümleri geliştiriyoruz.`
- Explanatory line: `İhtiyacınıza özel web platformları, mobil uygulamalar ve akıllı otomasyon çözümlerini uçtan uca geliştiriyoruz.`
- Primary CTA: `Projenizi Konuşalım`; secondary: `Projelerimizi İnceleyin`.

## Project credibility
Use real product UI screenshots, verify customer approval and ownership, distinguish client projects from team research/academics and internal R&D. Case studies should show the problem, solution, actual product and verified outcomes; include technical stack as a secondary layer.

## Future-ready, not premature
Future addition may use `/urunler` and `/urunler/[slug]`, with real product demos/pricing/waitlist where appropriate. Do not build product sales infrastructure now merely to signal ambition.
