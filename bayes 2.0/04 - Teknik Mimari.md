# 03 — Website Information Architecture and Content

> Kaynak: Kullanıcının ZIP'i / docs/03-website-architecture.md. Güncel durum ve bu turdaki kapsam için [[07 - AI Handoff]] ve [[08 - Phase 1 Tasarım Önerisi]] okunur. Mevcut Obsidian kasası kullanıcı kararıyla bayes 2.0/ konumunda korunur.


## Phase 1 constraint
Do not rebuild pages, change public routes, delete existing portfolio, rewrite AI/Web/Mobile component or add SaaS navigation. Audit project folders first.

## Proposed near-term top nav
- Home
- Services
- Projects / Case Studies
- About
- Contact with primary inquiry CTA

## Proposed longer-term IA (not now)
Add Products and product detail routes **only after a real ownable product is available**; optionally separate Labs/R&D from client deliveries later.

## Candidate homepage content order
1. Hero: clear capability message, primary `Projenizi Konuşalım` and secondary portfolio CTA.
2. Interactive Web/Mobile/AI showcase: explain actual service categories (Phase 2 rebuild).
3. Services: custom business software, corporate sites/web platforms, mobile, AI/integrations, backend/cloud as fitting existing offerings.
4. Large featured client project (Tam Finans candidate, ownership and claims verified).
5. Selected 2–3 strong case studies with actual screenshots.
6. Why BayesSoft: compact, proof-based differentiation.
7. Team of five: real photo/bios, optional professional profile links.
8. Contact CTA/footer.

Previously proposed longer 9-section flow also had a standalone SaaS Products showcase and process block. **This was superseded when user clarified no current SaaS product.** Process can be a short strip or service detail rather than a major repetitive homepage block.

## Content writing guidelines
- Problem → Solution → Evidence → Technical details.
- Replace vague/unverified claims, client logos and metrics with approved factual material.
- Portfolio types should be accurate; no invented business results.
- Form should be mobile friendly, clear status/errors, secure spam handling and privacy compliance; WhatsApp as optional contact convenience, only if desired and staffed.

## Technical verification checklist
Metadata/title descriptions, Open Graph, canonical/sitemap/robots, semantic structure, responsive layout, image/font loading, accessible focus/contrast, reduced-motion and form tracking. No existing audit numbers available; do not invent.
