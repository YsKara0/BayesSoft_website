## General evaluation
Earlier ChatGPT gave an **editorial score 7.4/10**, broken down as positioning 8.5, content and service clarity 8, project presentation 8, conversion 6, trust 7, differentiation 7. This was expressly not Lighthouse or exhaustive live-browser visual testing. These scores are only contextual.

## Positives
- Engineering-oriented positioning rather than a generic template/web agency.
- Team of five visible; brand story potentially authentic.
- Varied body of work across finance, health, logistics, consumer app and AI/research.
- Hero/Web→Mobile→AI ambition is a differentiator if clearly executed.

## Improvement ideas
1. Hero copy to explain the exact deliverable and value faster. Consider `Dijital fikirleri güçlü yazılımlara dönüştürüyoruz.` with explanatory service line and 2 CTAs.
2. Show genuine screens/functional software rather than abstract imagery. Large featured work; 2–3 impactful projects, not crowded small cards.
3. Case studies: customer problem, delivered solution, real visuals, outcomes, tech details secondary. Mark status/ownership.
4. Authentically tell founding team story; LinkedIn/GitHub optional.
5. Permissioned clients/testimonials only; avoid fake trust counters or generic social proof.
6. Contact: clear offer, low-friction form; optional WhatsApp, clear submit/error states, spam protection and privacy compliance.
7. SEO: differentiated service pages, correct metadata, sitemap, performance, mobile readability and accessible motion.
8. Avoid repeating Web/Mobile/AI value statement in multiple large homepage sections.
