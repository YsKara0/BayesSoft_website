## 0. Why this brief exists

The previous BayesSoft 4.0 directions A (Editorial Precision), B (Engineered Minimalism), and C (Digital Atelier) were clean but too basic. They felt like safe, generic startup templates, not a memorable design for a boutique software studio. B offered the most promising structural discipline; **do not copy its device icons, centered SaaS hero, four boxed services, or other weak executions**. The identity board's thin four-line motif was not understood or liked. Manrope was less successful than the Instrument Sans used in BayesSoft 3.0. Existing project screenshots, especially low-quality FurtherUp Drive captures, weakened quality. Do not use them.

This is the **last deliberate Figma creative-direction experiment before selecting a template and starting real frontend implementation**. Spend effort on art direction, not on generating many options or writing long documents.

## 1. The one central creative idea

**BUILT AROUND YOU — Size göre şekillenen teknoloji.**

BayesSoft develops tailored web, mobile, AI and custom software solutions for SMEs and startups. Its identity should evoke precise systems thoughtfully shaped around different requirements. The metaphor is **designed structure / adaptive spatial composition**, not a generic floating sculpture or puzzle.

Develop a distinctive, original **hero signature**: a restrained geometric/spatial arrangement that seems carefully engineered *for a purpose*—an architectural digital construction with clear visual rhythm, precise junctions, planes and depth. It should support the message of custom-fit technology without needing a paragraph to explain it. Aim for the visual sophistication of a premium design/technology studio, not a corporate presentation.

Crucial: If this metaphor produces only another anonymous abstract object, abandon that specific execution and use a better editorial typographic composition. **Brand relevance and powerful composition outrank forced 3D.**

## 2. Non-negotiable direction

- **Minimalist but surprising:** generous intentional whitespace, expressive scale, extremely careful alignments, distinctive rhythm and editorial confidence.
- **Premium yet approachable:** credible software studio, not luxury fashion parody or cold banking software.
- **Original, identifiable and usable:** work should remain compelling with motion off and logo temporarily hidden.
- **Not dark/green-dominant:** predominantly white or cool-neutral backgrounds; dark navy/graphite for contrast; saturated blue used sparingly for focus and interaction.
- **No main green palette.** Avoid green-tinted mineral gray, moss/olive, teal-green, or the heavy dark teal from option C.
- **Instrument Sans first:** use the approved BayesSoft 3.0 font as first choice; check actual file/usage and Turkish glyph coverage. Choose deliberate weight/size/line-height and sensible fallbacks. Do not default to Manrope.
- **Sparse, well-crafted Turkish:** short headlines, no filler jargon, no long paragraphs, avoid false performance/customer claims.
- Four offerings balanced: Web, Mobil, Yapay zekâ, Özel yazılım.

## 3. Composition quality requirements

Create a **coherent complete homepage** with one powerful hero; ensure each section visually belongs to the same identity rather than looking like separate UI kits.

The opening frame must have a recognizable point of view: confident editorial title + original brand-led visual system. Avoid the common composition of a centered 2-line heading, tiny subtitle, button and generic illustration underneath. Avoid four nearly identical cards, devices sitting on a shelf, arbitrary line decorations, indistinct geometric blocks and conventional SaaS template spacing.

Use strict optical alignment, deliberate asymmetry where appropriate, bold scale changes, negative space as structure, sophisticated visual cropping and planned transitions between sections. Do not sacrifice readability for novelty. Avoid ornamental grids drawn only for decoration; the underlying layout grid should be precise but mostly invisible.

### Required visible sections

1. **Header:** verified wordmark/logo, lean real navigation labels, one understated but clear inquiry action; aligned to composition.
2. **Hero:** outstanding art-directed design with a strong Turkish headline, max one short support sentence, clear CTA, and an original spatial/graphic hero signature designed from scratch in Figma with editable components.
3. **Capabilities:** all four services legible and balanced, with a purposeful editorial treatment, not a generic card grid.
4. **Selected work / capability evidence:** a visually distinctive project-presentation pattern **without importing any project screenshots or imagery**. Use expressive project titles, concise verified or generic-but-clearly-placeholder descriptors, and typographic/image-free placeholder frames that still look intentional. Do not imply a fake project or invent client outcomes. Never use FurtherUp Drive screenshots. Do not fetch assets from `assets`, `public`, prior Figma project images, or the web for project previews.
5. **Approach:** concise indication of bespoke process/working together, if it improves narrative.
6. **Contact transition + footer:** coherent ending and clear next step.

This is a **real website design**, not a brand board, slide deck, wireframe, diagram of the design system, or motion annotation as the main visual.

## 4. Hero art direction — originality first

Original hero visualization should be composed of Figma-editable geometry, masks, layering and restrained shading; no generic downloaded hero artwork, generated stock-looking asset, borrowed third-party composition, or unexplained random 3D sculpture.

Think about precise engineered relationships: planes meeting at purposeful interfaces, coordinated sections adapting to one container, an ordered transformation implied by a static composition. The graphic system must have clear scale and focal point. Prefer one strong visual sentence to ten small accents.

Avoid literal phones/laptops, hovering rounded cubes, abstract glowing orbs, brain/AI nodes, puzzle pieces, meaningless blueprint wireframes, fake app UI, and the disliked thin four-line motif.

**Motion readiness:** Identify at most 2–3 important places where motion would add meaning. Specify initial visual state, final visual state, trigger, duration range, likely CSS/GSAP/Three.js implementation, reduced-motion fallback. These are concise external annotations. Do not let motion be an excuse for weak static design. If hero art needs a complex animated realization later, clearly distinguish Figma mock representation from the future executable animation.

## 5. Typography and copy

Primary type: Instrument Sans, verifying the actual project source and Turkish characters. Make typography an expressive visual component, not default text on a blank page. Large hero line breaks must be intentional. Body should be restrained and readable. Use natural Turkish. Candidate message directions for exploration (choose/rewrite ONE, do not plaster multiple):

- `İşinize göre. Tam gerektiği gibi.`
- `Yazılım, sizin işinize uyum sağlasın.`
- `Fikirden çalışan sisteme.`

Do not blindly use any line if it feels weak. Avoid exaggerated claims, fake testimonials, invented numbers.

## 7. Creative gate — evaluate before delivering

Self-review the single concept against these questions:

1. Does the hero look memorably designed rather than like a conventional SaaS template?
2. Does the distinctive visual have a credible relationship to tailored software rather than being an unrelated artwork?
3. Is it still strong with animation turned off?
4. Can a visitor identify the offer and an action in five seconds?
5. Are all four services represented without excessive repetition?
6. Is the palette cool-neutral and blue, with NO green-dominant look?
7. Does Instrument Sans feel intentional and well-set?
8. Is the project section polished WITHOUT using any existing project screenshot?
9. Does the mobile version preserve the idea, not just the pixels?
10. Are hierarchy, alignment, whitespace, contrast, Turkish copy, accessibility and visual rhythm thoughtfully resolved?

If the first composition is weak, refine **that single concept**, not three entirely new ones. Prefer improving art direction over spending time on exhaustive automated testing. One final visual comparison is enough. No fabricated scoring or claims of guaranteed 'wow'.
