# BayesSoft 4.0 — Flagship Revision 02

**Status:** Final focused Figma art-direction experiment; review required before implementation.  
**Deliverable:** One distinctive desktop homepage + one coherent mobile homepage + optional hero visual detail.  
**Work area:** `feature/bayessoft-4.0`, Figma only, plus concise Obsidian logging.

## 01 — Why this revision exists

The previous *Built Around You / Yazılım, size göre.* direction moved us closer to the desired brand: restrained blue, off-white surfaces, dark type, Instrument Sans, and a cleaner layout. But it still feels like a generic corporate consultancy website rather than a contemporary software studio. The hero's folded frame does not immediately communicate anything; large portions of the page are copy-led and visually quiet without being memorable.

**Do not merely polish the existing folded frame.** Keep the approved foundations, but redesign the flagship composition and invent a more legible, technology-native visual centerpiece. This is one concentrated creative attempt, not three versions and not another brand board.

## 02 — Success criteria (all must be evident in the actual frames)

1. **First three seconds:** Without reading small text, a viewer recognizes a modern software/product engineering studio.
2. **Memorability:** The hero contains one identifiable, purposeful visual gesture; not stock 3D and not generic geometry.
3. **Visual > verbal:** The page carries its message primarily through composition, typography, visual rhythm, and distinct visual specimens.
4. **Minimalism with character:** Fewer elements, but better proportion, negative space, typography, materials and intentional asymmetry.
5. **Believable buildability:** The visual can plausibly be implemented later in SVG/CSS/GSAP or Three.js, including responsive and reduced-motion states.
6. **No misleading portfolio:** Do not invent real client testimonials, project outcomes, metrics, or product screenshots.

If the result could be reused unchanged by a law office, architectural practice or management consultancy, the design is not ready.

## 03 — Brand anchor

BayesSoft is a boutique software studio for SMEs and startups, combining four equally important capabilities:

- Web
- Mobile
- Artificial Intelligence
- Custom Software

**Core idea:** "İhtiyaca göre tasarlanan, birlikte çalışan yazılım."  
This is a *design rationale*, not mandatory hero copy. Choose short, natural Turkish wording. Do not force the words "size göre" into a physically awkward visual trick. The identity should communicate **precision, adaptability, integration and engineering craft**.

Visual tone: quiet high-tech; contemporary digital studio; carefully engineered; human and accessible; not futuristic science fiction; not sterile corporate consulting.

## 04 — Locked visual foundations

- **Primary type:** Instrument Sans. Use the existing font/license asset when appropriate; do not revert to Manrope. Strong typographic contrast through sizing, placement and spacing instead of many font families.
- **Palette:** White `#FFFFFF`, cool light surface `#F3F5F7`, cool pale blue-gray `#E9EEF3`, deep ink `#17252F`, secondary text `#536270`, accent blue `#285BD6`, pale blue `#DDE7FF`.
- **No green or green-cast mineral backdrop.** No green as dominant accent.
- Favor a light canvas with decisive dark and blue accents. Dark sections are allowed sparingly if they produce clear composition; do not reproduce Option C's full dark corporate block by default.
- Turkish-first copy with clean diacritics and deliberate line breaks.
- Existing BayesSoft name / verified wordmark. Do not redesign the logo now.

## 05 — Creative centerpiece: 'A working digital system'

**Propose one original visual centerpiece that looks purpose-built for a software studio, not a prop in an architecture showroom.**

Suggested starting vocabulary (not a mandatory object): layered computational planes, connected interface surfaces, an abstracted software workspace, precise spatial panels with meaningful changing states, intelligently arranged windows/modules whose visual logic implies input → transformation → useful output. The viewer should perceive **a system doing something**, not four inert objects sitting on a pedestal.

The centerpiece may be an editable vector/isometric composition in Figma, a tasteful original 3D-looking visual, or a combination of typography and spatial geometry. The final implementation can later use Three.js, but do not develop WebGL in this task.

**Concept rule:** Write a private one-sentence explanation of *what the visual represents* before drawing it. If its meaning depends on a paragraph next to it, simplify or change the design.

**Critical:** Avoid repeating the old empty folded-frame/open-rectangle as the sole hero motif; avoid a floating cuboid, random blue blobs, neon nodes, stock-style dashboard cards, generic phones/laptops, puzzle pieces and ornamental lines. The centerpiece must have scale, material finish, compositional intention and a functional metaphor.

Aim for a visual hero where **the object/scene and typography share the composition**; not simply 'headline on left + isolated illustration on right.' A controlled overlap, framing relationship or thoughtful asymmetry is welcome if readability remains high.

## 06 — Homepage information architecture and copy limits

Design the **entire homepage**, but make every section visual and concise. Use only verified existing company facts.

### Header

Verified BayesSoft identity, small number of useful links, one discreet but visible contact CTA. Crisp alignment; no oversized nav, pointless language tabs, or dead links. If destination validity is unknown, annotate it as a placeholder outside the page frame.

### Hero — most important

- One striking Turkish headline: preferably 3–7 words, maximum 2–3 visual lines.
- One supporting sentence: ideally 9–15 words, hard limit 20.
- One primary CTA with specific intent (e.g., `Projenizi konuşalım`).
- One integrated **purposeful** spatial/digital composition occupying a substantial visual share of the viewport.
- No split headline where an isolated word floats confusingly inside a box. The entire headline must be comprehensible immediately.
- No nested boxes masquerading as high art. No redundant badges or irrelevant tech buzzwords.

### Four capabilities

Present Web / Mobil / Yapay zekâ / Özel yazılım with equal weight. Prefer visual taxonomy or a single strong compositional treatment over four generic white cards. Each capability has at most a very short supporting phrase, or none if clear.

### Selected work / proof

Show **two original conceptual project-presentation slots**, not real project imagery. These are *layout studies* that can later accept real approved case-study assets. Design them as sophisticated typographic / color / geometric cover systems; visibly label as **`Görsel yer tutucu — gerçek proje görseli eklenecek`** in small design annotations outside the website frame. Do not label fabricated content as completed customer work. Do not use FurtherUp Drive or any other existing client screenshot, cropped real website, mockup screenshot, fake numerical performance metric or fabricated testimonial.

If confirmed real project names are available, names may be used with carefully limited factual descriptors; otherwise use neutral placeholders like `Proje 01` and `Proje 02`.

### Process / approach

One concise proposition or a three-step process represented visually. Avoid three paragraphs and broad generic claims.

### Final contact + footer

One strong, short contact invitation, a clear action, restrained footer. No unnecessary duplicate CTAs stacked across sections.

**Copy budget:** Compared with the prior flagship direction, visibly reduce total body-copy density by approximately 40–60%. This is a qualitative layout target, not a character-count hack. Never reduce legibility to meet a quota.

## 07 — Art-direction craft rules

- Make the first viewport arresting through hierarchy, spacing and an original scene, **not** sheer quantity of elements.
- Use deliberate asymmetry and precise grid alignment; maintain visual cadence across sections.
- Make visual objects large enough to feel intentional, but keep typography clearly readable.
- Keep sophisticated materials restrained: matte white, pale blue/gray, selectively dark surfaces, subtle physically plausible shadow and controlled specular response.
- Avoid green-tinted gray, generic SaaS feature grids, tiny distant visuals, floating device icons, clip-art, gimmicky angled cards, heavy glow, gradients used without rationale, fake depth and visual noise.
- Don't fill whitespace with more copy. Redistribute scale and composition instead.
- Ensure page does not become an architecture or construction firm site: the designed artifacts must suggest digital/software behavior.
- Do not add unverified awards, clients, logos, numbers, testimonials or capabilities.

## 08 — Motion, designed now and implemented later

For the hero visual, explicitly show or annotate:

1. **Static keyframe A** — clear at first view.
2. **Static keyframe B** — how a purposeful system changes state, e.g. inputs or modules resolve into an integrated software surface.
3. **Motion rationale** — what changes, why, trigger, approx duration and candidate tech (CSS/SVG/GSAP/Three.js).
4. **Reduced-motion fallback** — static final composition.

Keep these notes **outside** the finished website frames. Do not imply that the Figma canvas itself proves animation quality. Do not design any movement that is impossible or nonsensical without continuous looping. Motion should not be required to understand what BayesSoft does.

## 10 — Internal quality gate (one focused pass; no endless iteration)

Check the finished desktop at full-page scale and at the first-screen viewport. Ask:

- Is this recognizably a modern software studio within 3 seconds?
- Does the hero visual convey system, transformation or digital product engineering without explanatory paragraphs?
- Is the entire headline naturally readable?
- Is the scene impactful yet restrained, with a clear focal point?
- Are four services balanced and concise?
- Are projects honest placeholders, with no existing client screenshots?
- Is the layout distinctive from prior A/B/C and previous flagship?
- Does mobile have a genuinely coherent visual composition?

If any crucial criterion fails, make **one targeted correction**. Do not spawn another three-concept cycle.
